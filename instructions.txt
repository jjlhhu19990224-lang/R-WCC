You must have already installed Microsoft Office 2003 or 2007 before installing the following:
(You will need administrator priviledges to install these)
1.) Run owc11.exe (this is the Office Web Components plugin), follow the dialogs
2.) Run owc2007sp1-kb937961-fullfile-en-us.exe (this is the OWC Service Pack for Office 2007), follow the dialogs
3.) You may or may not have to reboot
4.) you can now run wcc.exe

If you ever reinstall or upgrade Office, you may have to repeat step 1 and 2.


To use the program:

1.) Click File->Open
2.) Open example_dyad_data.txt
3.) Set Window and MaxLag values as needed and click the Apply button
3.) Click on the visualization to display the associated portions of the function being compared in the bottom chart, and to display the respective correlation value in the bottom right-hand corner.
